Build alongside the Community in person
Join us at Builders & Brews: Hack Edition , a global series of in-person meetups co-hosted by Nebius and Tavily. Meet the teams in person, connect with fellow builders, find teammates, join technical workshops, and get hands-on support from mentors as you turn your idea into a working project. Attendees can also unlock additional Nebius AI Cloud, Token Factory, Tavily credits to keep building after the event.
Events are coming to participating cities across the Americas, Asia-Pacific, Europe, and the Middle East. You don’t need to attend an event to enter the hackathon or qualify for a $500 City Winner Award —all eligible submissions associated with a participating city will be considered. But if you want to meet the community, get unstuck, and build alongside others, we’d love to see you there.
